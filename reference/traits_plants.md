# Functional Traits of Fleshy-fruit plants along a Tropical Gradient

Taxonomy and functional traits of 392 fleshy-fruited plant species from
the Manu National Park in south-east Peru. Given are fruit length and
width (mm), plant height (m) and crop mass (g). Fruit traits have been
measured on fresh fruit samples. Number of fruits per plant (used to
determine the crop mass) and plant height have been estimated in the
field. Species names are indicated as row names.

## Usage

``` r
traits_plants
```

## Format

A data frame with 392 rows and 4 variables:

- Fruit.length..mm.:

  fruit length, in mm

- Fruit.width..mm.:

  fruit width, in mm

- Plant.height..m.:

  plant height, in m

- Crop.mass..g.:

  seed mass, in g

## Source

Nowak, Larissa et al. (2019), Data from: Projecting consequences of
global warming for the functional diversity of fleshy-fruited plants and
frugivorous birds along a tropical elevational gradient, Dryad, Dataset,
[doi:10.5061/dryad.c0n737b](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.c0n737b)
